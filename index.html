Google Drive Backup Tool

Welcome to the Google Drive Backup Tool information page. This tool is designed to help its owner back up selected items to their Google Drive account.

Purpose

The application uses Google authorization to access the Google services required to perform backup operations. It is intended to make personal data backup and storage easier.

Data and Privacy

Google account access is used only for the backup functionality intended by the application. The application does not intend to sell personal information or use Google account data for advertising.

For details about data handling, access, and privacy, please read our Privacy Policy.
