GC Backup

GC Backup is a backup utility that helps users copy selected items to their own Google Drive accounts.

What GC Backup Does

The application uses Google authorization to access the Google Drive features required for its backup functionality. Users authorize access through Google's authorization process and use the application to perform their intended backup tasks.

Data and Privacy

GC Backup is designed to use Google account data only as necessary to provide its backup functionality. The data accessed depends on the permissions granted by the user and the backup operation being performed.

For details about data access, use, storage, sharing, and deletion, please read our Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

Google Drive and Google's authorization services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about GC Backup or its privacy practices, contact the application operator using the contact details provided in the Privacy Policy.
